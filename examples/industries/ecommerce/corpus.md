# file: returns.md
## Return window
Most items can be returned within 10 days of delivery. Electronics have a 7 day replacement-only window, and innerwear, perishables and personalised items cannot be returned.
## Refund timeline
Refunds are issued after the returned item passes a quality check at the warehouse. Money reaches UPI and wallets within 2 days and cards within 5 to 7 working days.
## Return pickup
Request a return pickup from My Orders. A courier collects the item within 2 days; keep it in its original packaging with all tags attached.

# file: shipping.md
## Delivery times
Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 days in metro cities and up to 8 days elsewhere. Express delivery reaches metro cities the next day if you order before 2 pm.
## Delivery charges
Orders above 499 rupees ship free. Smaller orders pay a delivery charge of 40 rupees, and express delivery costs 99 rupees extra.
## Cash on delivery
Cash on delivery is available on orders up to 10,000 rupees. A handling fee of 20 rupees applies to every cash on delivery order.

# file: payments.md
## Payment methods
ShopKart accepts UPI, debit and credit cards, net banking, wallets and cash on delivery. No-cost EMI is available on cards for orders above 3,000 rupees.
## Failed payments
If money is debited but the order fails, it is reversed automatically within 3 working days. Contact support with the transaction reference if it does not arrive.
## Gift cards
Gift cards are valid for 1 year from the date of issue and cannot be exchanged for cash. Up to 3 gift cards can be used on a single order.

# file: membership.md
## ShopKart Plus benefits
ShopKart Plus members get free express delivery, early access to sales, and 2 percent extra cashback on every order. Membership costs 799 rupees a year.
## Cancelling membership
Cancel Plus within 15 days of joining for a full refund if you have not used any benefit. After that, the membership runs until the end of the year.
## Seller offers
