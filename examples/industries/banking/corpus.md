# file: savings-account.md
## Interest rate
The Northwind Savings Account pays interest of 3.5 percent per year on balances up to 5 lakh rupees and 4 percent on the part above 5 lakh. Interest is calculated on the daily closing balance and credited every quarter.
## Minimum balance
Savings accounts in metro branches must keep an average monthly balance of 10,000 rupees. Rural branches need 2,000 rupees. If the average monthly balance falls short, a fee of 300 rupees plus GST is charged.
## Opening an account
You can open a savings account online with your PAN and Aadhaar through video KYC, which takes about 15 minutes. Minors can hold an account operated by a parent or guardian.
## Closing an account
Accounts closed within 14 days of opening are free of charge. Closing an account between 14 days and one year after opening costs 500 rupees. After one year there is no closure fee.

# file: fixed-deposits.md
## Fixed deposit rates
Fixed deposits from 7 days to 10 years are available. A deposit of 1 to 2 years earns 7.1 percent per year. Senior citizens earn an extra 0.5 percent on every tenure.
## Premature withdrawal
Breaking a fixed deposit before maturity carries a penalty of 1 percent on the interest rate for the period the deposit actually ran. Tax-saver fixed deposits have a 5 year lock-in and cannot be broken early.
## Tax on fixed deposit interest
Tax is deducted at source when fixed deposit interest exceeds 40,000 rupees in a financial year, or 50,000 rupees for senior citizens. Submit Form 15G or 15H to avoid the deduction if your income is below the taxable limit.

# file: loans.md
## Home loan eligibility
Salaried applicants aged 21 to 60 with at least 2 years of work experience can apply for a home loan. The bank lends up to 80 percent of the property value for loans above 30 lakh rupees.
## Home loan interest
Home loan interest starts at 8.6 percent per year on a floating rate linked to the repo rate. The rate is reset every 3 months.
## Loan prepayment
There is no prepayment charge on floating rate home loans for individual borrowers. Fixed rate loans carry a prepayment charge of 2 percent of the amount prepaid.
## Personal loans
Personal loans range from 50,000 rupees to 25 lakh rupees for 1 to 5 years. Pre-approved customers receive the money in their account within 4 hours.

# file: cards-and-fraud.md
## Lost or stolen card
Block a lost or stolen debit or credit card instantly in the mobile app under Cards, then Block card. You can also call the 24x7 helpline. A replacement card is delivered in 5 to 7 working days.
## Unauthorised transactions
Report an unauthorised transaction within 3 working days to have zero liability. Reports made within 4 to 7 working days limit your liability to 10,000 rupees. The bank credits a disputed amount within 10 working days while it investigates.
