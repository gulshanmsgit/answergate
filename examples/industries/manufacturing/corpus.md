# file: product-carbon-footprint.md
## Footprint method
Product carbon footprints are calculated cradle-to-gate following ISO 14067, using the bill of materials, supplier energy data and the ecoinvent database. Each footprint is reviewed by an external verifier every 2 years.
## Steel bracket footprint
The SB-200 steel bracket has a footprint of 4.2 kg CO2e per unit, of which 78 percent comes from the steel. Switching to steel with 60 percent recycled content cuts it to 2.6 kg CO2e.
## Aluminium housing footprint
The AH-50 aluminium housing has a footprint of 11.8 kg CO2e per unit. Using low-carbon aluminium smelted with hydropower reduces it by about 45 percent.

# file: quality.md
## Inspection
Every production batch is sampled under ISO 2859-1 at general inspection level II. A batch is rejected when defects exceed the acceptance number for its sample size.
## Defect targets
The plant targets fewer than 500 defective parts per million shipped. The figure for the last quarter was 320 parts per million.
## Returns and corrective action
Customer returns for a quality defect get an 8D corrective action report within 10 working days. Containment starts within 24 hours of the complaint.

# file: supplier-code.md
## Supplier requirements
Suppliers must hold ISO 9001 certification and report their energy use and emissions every year. New suppliers are audited on site before their first order.
## Conflict minerals
Suppliers must declare tin, tantalum, tungsten and gold sources with the conflict minerals reporting template. Smelters must be on the conformant list.

# file: lead-times.md
## Standard lead times
Standard catalogue parts ship in 3 weeks. Custom parts need 6 to 8 weeks, including 2 weeks for tooling.
## Minimum order quantity
The minimum order quantity is 500 units for catalogue parts and 2,000 units for custom parts.
## Expedited orders
Orders can be expedited by 1 week for a surcharge of 15 percent, subject to capacity.

## Safety record
The plant has recorded a lost time injury frequency rate of 0.4 per million hours worked over the last 12 months. Every worker completes safety training before starting on the line.
## Waste and water
82 percent of production waste is recycled. Process water is treated on site and reused, cutting fresh water use by 35 percent since 2022.
