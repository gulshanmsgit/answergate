# file: motor-policy.md
## Own damage cover
The comprehensive motor policy covers damage to your car from accidents, fire, theft, floods and riots. Wear and tear, mechanical breakdown and driving without a valid licence are not covered.
## No claim bonus
A no claim bonus of 20 percent is earned after one claim-free year, rising to 50 percent after five claim-free years. The bonus stays with you when you change your car.
## Zero depreciation add-on
The zero depreciation add-on pays the full cost of replaced parts without deducting depreciation. It is available for cars up to 5 years old and covers 2 claims a year.

# file: health-policy.md
## Waiting periods
Health policies have a 30 day initial waiting period for illnesses, except accidents. Pre-existing diseases are covered after 3 years, and named surgeries such as cataract after 2 years.
## Room rent limit
Room rent is covered up to 1 percent of the sum insured per day, and intensive care up to 2 percent. Choosing a costlier room reduces the claim proportionately.
## Pre and post hospitalisation
Medical expenses 30 days before and 60 days after a hospital admission are covered when they relate to the same illness.

# file: claims.md
## Reporting a claim
Report a motor claim within 48 hours of the incident in the SecureLife app or by calling the claims helpline. Theft must also be reported to the police with a first information report.
## Cashless claims
Cashless claims are settled directly with network garages and hospitals. Reimbursement claims must be filed within 30 days of discharge or repair with original bills.
## Claim settlement time
Most claims are settled within 7 working days of receiving all documents. If settlement is delayed beyond 30 days, interest is paid at 2 percent above the bank rate.

# file: renewals.md
## Grace period
Policies can be renewed up to 30 days after expiry for health and 90 days for the no claim bonus on motor policies. Health cover is not active during the grace period.
## Free look period
New health policies have a free look period of 30 days. If you cancel within it, the premium is refunded after deducting the cost of medical tests and cover used.
