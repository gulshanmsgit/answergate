# file: leave-policy.md
## Annual leave
Full-time employees get 24 days of paid annual leave a year, credited at 2 days a month. Up to 10 unused days carry over to the next year; the rest lapse on 31 March.
## Sick leave
Employees get 12 days of paid sick leave a year. A doctor's certificate is needed for sick leave longer than 2 consecutive days.
## Parental leave
Birth mothers get 26 weeks of paid maternity leave. Other parents, including adoptive parents, get 4 weeks of paid parental leave within 6 months of the child's arrival.
## Applying for leave
Apply for leave in the HR portal at least 7 days ahead for planned leave. Your manager approves or declines within 2 working days.

# file: work-from-home.md
## Hybrid schedule
Employees work from the office at least 3 days a week. Teams agree on their office days with their manager.
## Home office allowance
A one-time home office allowance of 15,000 rupees covers a chair, desk or monitor. Claim it in the expense portal with receipts within 60 days of joining.
## Working from another city
Working from another city for more than 2 weeks needs written approval from your manager and HR. Working from another country is not allowed.

# file: payroll-and-benefits.md
## Salary date
Salaries are credited on the last working day of each month. Payslips are available in the HR portal on the same day.
## Health cover
Employees, their spouse, children and parents are covered by group health insurance of 5 lakh rupees a year. Employees can top up cover for parents at their own cost.
## Reimbursements
Submit expense claims within 30 days with receipts. Approved claims are paid with the next month's salary.

# file: learning.md
## Learning budget
Each employee has a learning budget of 40,000 rupees a year for courses, certifications and conferences. Unused budget does not carry over.
## Mentorship
New joiners are paired with a mentor for their first 90 days. Mentors meet their mentees at least once every two weeks.
